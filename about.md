<section class="about-intro">
  <div class="intro-content">
    <p>
      ChristIAM- isn't a brand. It isn't a company. It isn't a ministry in the traditional sense. It's a testimony that grew legs — a story God told in one man's life that's still being written, still being built, still becoming.
    </p>
    <p>
      The name carries three truths:
    </p>
    <ul class="name-breakdown">
      <li><strong>Christ</strong> — the One who reached down into the pit.</li>
      <li><strong>IAM</strong> — the name God gave Himself at the burning bush. "I AM who I AM." Exodus 3:14.</li>
      <li><strong>-</strong> — the dash. The hyphen. The in-between. The space between who I was and who I'm becoming.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      Everything here flows from that name. Every project, every app, every page — they're all rooms in the same house, built by the same hand, pointing to the same Source.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-story">
  <h2>The Man</h2>
  <div class="story-content">
    <p>
      Will Joshua Shumate. A name that wasn't chosen — it was given. <em>Joshua</em> is the Hebrew <em>Yeshua</em>, the same name the angel gave to Mary's child. "The Lord saves." It's not a title earned. It's an inheritance received.
    </p>
    <p>
      Before the inheritance, there was the pit. Years of IV drug abuse. A heart pushed past every limit. A rage so deep it turned self-destruction into a philosophy. A heart attack on a floor with the devil standing overhead, waiting.
    </p>
    <p>
      And then — forgiveness. One plea. One word. And God reached down and lifted him out.
    </p>
    <blockquote class="scripture-block">
      "He lifted me out of the slimy pit, out of the mud and mire; he set my feet on a rock and gave me a firm place to stand." — Psalm 40:2
    </blockquote>
    <p>
      The full testimony is raw and unfiltered. No polish. No performance. Just the real, authentic IAM — the one who was in the pit and the One who pulled him out.
    </p>
    <p>
      <a href="{{ '/' | relative_url }}#testimonies" class="text-link">Read the full testimony &rarr;</a>
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-mission">
  <h2>The Mission</h2>
  <div class="mission-content">
    <p>
      The mission is simple: bear fruit that lasts.
    </p>
    <blockquote class="scripture-block">
      "You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you might go and bear fruit — fruit that will last." — John 15:16
    </blockquote>
    <p>
      That fruit takes many forms:
    </p>
    <div class="mission-grid">
      <div class="mission-item">
        <h3>The Testimony</h3>
        <p>Telling the story — the pit, the rescue, the name. Raw and unfiltered. So that anyone in exile knows the door is open.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="mission-item">
        <h3>The Foundation</h3>
        <p>Clean water. Bathhouses and laundry facilities in remote and low-income areas. 2,950+ people served across 6 countries — and counting.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="mission-item">
        <h3>The Gallery</h3>
        <p>Art as spiritual warfare. 11 pieces, each one a demon faced and overcome. Despair, self-hatred, isolation — transformed on canvas.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="mission-item">
        <h3>The Community</h3>
        <p>The misfits. The black sheep. The ones who don't fit anywhere else. Devotionals, prayer, and a place to belong.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-beliefs">
  <h2>What I Believe</h2>
  <div class="beliefs-content">
    <p>
      I believe in one God — the I AM. The One who was and is and is to come. The One who doesn't wait for us to be worthy before He calls us by name.
    </p>
    <p>
      I believe in transformation. Not self-improvement, not behavior modification — a new heart. Stone out. Flesh in. The old gone, the new coming.
    </p>
    <p>
      I believe the testimony has power. That the words of those who've been through the fire and come out the other side can reach someone still in the flames.
    </p>
    <p>
      I believe in the misfits. The ones the world threw away. The black sheep. The exiles. Because that's who I was — and that's who He came for.
    </p>
    <blockquote class="scripture-block">
      "And they have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony." — Revelation 12:11
    </blockquote>
