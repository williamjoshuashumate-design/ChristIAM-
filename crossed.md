<section class="gallery-intro">
  <div class="intro-content">
    <p>
      CROSSED is a label built on one truth: the stitching is what holds the shirt together.
      On regular garments the thread hides inside the seams. On ours, it's the main theme —
      oversized, exposed, and unmistakable.
    </p>
    <p>
      Every seam is sewn with giant cross-stitch patterns in thick gold and cream yarn. Not
      decoration. Construction — made visible, so what holds you together becomes the point
      of the whole piece.
    </p>
    <blockquote class="scripture-block">
      <p>"IAMTHATIAM." — Exodus 3:14</p>
    </blockquote>
