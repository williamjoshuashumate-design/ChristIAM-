CO-OWNERSHIP PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

CROSSED Apparel Label — September 12, 2026

"The stitching is what holds it together."

1. Formation of Partnership

By this Agreement, William Joshua Shumate and Christopher Michael Shumate (the “Partners”) form a general partnership (the “Partnership”) to jointly own, operate, and grow the apparel label CROSSED, including its flagship designs, wordmarks, and all brand assets, on the terms below.

2. Name, Purpose, and Principal Place of Business

  1. Name. The Partnership shall operate under the trade name CROSSED.
  2. Purpose. The purpose of the Partnership is the design, production, marketing, sale, and distribution of apparel and related merchandise under the CROSSED label, including but not limited to heavyweight embroidered hoodies and tees, short-sleeve hoodies with open hand pockets, missions edition garments, performance boxer briefs, and future designs.
  3. Principal Place of Business. , or such other location as the Partners may agree in writing.

3. Partnership Property and Brand Assets

  1. Existing brand assets contributed. William Joshua Shumate contributes to the Partnership all right, title, and interest in the CROSSED brand assets created to date, including: the CROSSED name and label; the UnoWHO, IAMU, IAMTHATIAM, THE GREAT IAM., CHRISTIAM, and MESSIAM wordmarks and designs; all product design concepts, images, and design files; the /crossed/ and /iamu/ brand pages and related site content; and the “UnoWHO” design folder and all files it contains.
  2. Future assets. All brand assets, designs, trademarks, and intellectual property created for or by the Partnership shall be owned by the Partnership jointly and equally.
  3. Existing assets remain partnership property regardless of which Partner created them, and may not be sold, licensed, or encumbered except by unanimous written consent of the Partners.

4. Ownership Interests and Contributions

  1. Ownership. The Partners shall each hold a fifty percent (50%) interest in the Partnership, including all brand assets, property, and future value.
  2. Contribution of Christopher Michael Shumate. Christopher Michael Shumate contributes the sum of $ and/or the following services and resources: .
  3. Additional capital. Any additional capital contribution by either Partner requires unanimous consent and shall be recorded in writing.

5. Roles and Responsibilities

  1. William Joshua Shumate — Creative Director. Creative direction, brand identity, design of the label's garments and wordmarks, and brand communications.
  2. Christopher Michael Shumate — Business Operations Lead. Business operations, production and manufacturer relationships, fulfillment, and day-to-day administration, subject to Section 6.
  3. Each Partner is an equal co-owner with full access to Partnership books, records, accounts, and information. Each Partner shall act in good faith and in the best interest of the Partnership.

6. Management and Decision-Making

  1. Routine decisions. Either Partner may make routine day-to-day decisions within their role as described in Section 5, keeping the other reasonably informed.
  2. Major decisions require unanimous written consent of both Partners, including: (a) signing contracts or manufacturer agreements; (b) borrowing money or incurring debt; (c) selling, licensing, or encumbering brand assets or intellectual property; (d) admitting new partners; (e) setting prices; (f) expenditures above $; and (g) any change to the brand name, wordmarks, or core brand identity.
  3. Deadlock. If the Partners cannot agree on a major decision, they shall first attempt good-faith mediation before taking any irreversible action.

7. Profits, Losses, and Distributions

  1. All net profits and losses of the Partnership shall be allocated and distributed equally (50/50) between the Partners.
  2. Distributions shall be made on a schedule the Partners agree to in writing, after retaining reasonable reserves for inventory, production, taxes, and growth.
  3. Neither Partner shall take any Partnership funds for personal use except as authorized distributions or reasonable, agreed compensation for work performed.

8. Books, Records, and Bank Accounts

  1. The Partnership shall maintain accurate books of account, accessible to both Partners at all times.
  2. Partnership funds shall be kept in a Partnership bank account requiring both Partners' authorization for withdrawals above $.

9. Term, Withdrawal, Death, and Buyout

  1. Term. The Partnership begins on the date of the last signature below and continues until dissolved by mutual written agreement or as provided herein.
  2. Withdrawal. A Partner wishing to withdraw shall give sixty (60) days' written notice. The withdrawing Partner shall be bought out at the fair market value of their fifty percent (50%) interest, determined by a mutually agreed independent appraiser, payable on terms the Partners agree in writing.
  3. Right of First Refusal. Before selling any interest to a third party, the selling Partner must first offer it to the other Partner on the same terms.
  4. Death or incapacity. If a Partner dies or becomes permanently incapacitated, the other Partner shall have the right to purchase the deceased/incapacitated Partner's interest at fair market value, payable over a reasonable period, with the proceeds going to the Partner or their estate.

10. Confidentiality and Brand Protection

  1. Each Partner shall keep confidential all non-public Partnership information, designs, supplier terms, and business plans, both during and after the Partnership.
  2. Neither Partner shall use the brand assets, wordmarks, or designs for any outside venture without the other Partner's written consent, during or after the Partnership.

11. Dispute Resolution and Governing Law

  1. Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith discussion, then mediation with a mutually agreed mediator.
  2. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of .
  3. If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

12. Entire Agreement and Amendments

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Partners regarding the Partnership and may be amended only in writing signed by both Partners.
