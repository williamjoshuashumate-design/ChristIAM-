  <h1>The Gallery</h1>
  <p class="page-tagline">From Darkness to Light</p>
</section>

<section class="gallery-intro">
  <div class="intro-content">
    <p>
      Every piece in this gallery is a battle. Each one began in darkness — a demon faced, a lie believed, a weight carried. And each one ended in light — not because the darkness was defeated, but because it was <em>transformed.</em>
    </p>
    <p>
      The same rage that tried to destroy me became the fuel that feeds this art. The crimson is its blood. The gold is what I made of it.
    </p>
    <blockquote class="scripture-block">
      "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." — John 1:5
    </blockquote>
  </div>
</section>

      <p class="art-date">January 2026</p>
      <div class="art-demon">
        <span class="demon-label">Demon:</span>
        <span class="demon-name">The Demon of Despair</span>
      </div>
      <p class="art-story">This is the moment I felt the spark return. After everything went dark — after the demons had their way with my mind and my body — there was a light. Not outside of me, but within. A raw and God spark that had been there all along, buried under the weight of what I was carrying. I painted this from that place. The figure rising is me. The gold is not gold — it is grace. It is the thing that sustains you when you have nothing left to sustain yourself with.</p>
      <p class="art-overcoming">Despair told me there was no way out. It told me the darkness was permanent. But the spark within me — the same spark within you — does not die. It waits. And when you are ready to reach for it, it rises with you. Despair does not win. It cannot win, because it is not real. The spark is real.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="art-info">
      <h3>The Transformation</h3>
      <p class="art-date">February 2026</p>
      <div class="art-demon">
        <span class="demon-label">Demon:</span>
        <span class="demon-name">The Demon of Self-Hatred</span>
      </div>
      <p class="art-story">This is the demon I faced longest — the one that wore my face, spoke in my voice, and told me I was not worth saving. For years I believed it. I let it live inside me and make every decision. And then one day I stopped fighting it and started transforming it. I did not kill this demon. I could not. Instead, I turned it. I made it work for me. Now the very thing that tried to destroy me is the fuel that feeds this art. The crimson is its blood. The gold is what I made of it.</p>
      <p class="art-overcoming">Self-hatred is the loudest demon because it sounds like your own voice. But it is not your voice. Your real voice is the one that says "I am still here. I am still fighting." That voice is the God spark. Self-hatred tried to bury me. What it did not know is that I am a seed. Everything that tried to destroy me became the soil I grew from.</p>
    </div>
  </article>

      <p class="art-story">I painted this during the worst of it — when I was still in the dark and had not yet found the light. But my hands were reaching. I did not know what I was reaching for. I did not know if anything would reach back. But I reached anyway, because something in me — deeper than thought, deeper than fear — refused to stop. This piece is for anyone who is still reaching and has not yet felt the answer. Keep reaching. The spark is reaching for you too.</p>
      <p class="art-overcoming">Isolation is the lie that you are alone. You are not alone. Every person who has walked through darkness and come out the other side is reaching back for you. I am reaching back for you. The dark does not last. The spark within you is real, and it connects you to something greater than yourself. You are part of a fabric that cannot be torn.</p>
