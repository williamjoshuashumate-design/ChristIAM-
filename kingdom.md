  <h2>The Vision</h2>
  <div class="vision-content">
    <p>
      Every app in this kingdom is a room in the Father's house. Some are furnished and lived in — the testimony is told, the water is flowing, the art is on the walls. Others are still being prepared — empty rooms waiting for their purpose to be revealed.
    </p>
    <p>
      But none of them stand alone. The testimony feeds the foundation. The foundation feeds the art. The art feeds the community. The community feeds the testimony. It's a circle — a kingdom — and it all points back to the One who started it.
    </p>
    <p class="kingdom-verse">
      "You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you might go and bear fruit — fruit that will last." — John 15:16

    <p>
      This is the fruit. And it will last.
    </p>
